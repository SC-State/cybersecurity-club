What the name means

What IC CAE stands for

The name is long because it comes from a federal program. Here's the short version, and why it matters for your career.

IC
Intelligence Community

The federal agencies that collect and analyze intelligence for national security.

CAE
Centers of Academic Excellence

A program that partners with universities to prepare students for national security careers. SC State's Center of Excellence in Cybersecurity is a member.

Since 2020

Based in the Department of Computer Science and Mathematics.

25+ members

Active members, with room for more.

CAE-CD to 2029

NSA and DHS designate SC State a National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense.

How we train

Pick a side. Or both.

Red team

Think like an attacker, inside a lab where it's legal.
  • Capture-the-flag challenges
  • Web app attacks in a practice lab
  • Password cracking, and why long passwords win
  • Recon and open-source intelligence

Blue team

Keep systems running while someone tries to break them.
  • Network hardening and firewall rules
  • Log analysis and traffic monitoring
  • Incident response drills
  • Cyber defense competitions against other schools

Both sides

Everything the club was founded to do.
  • Technical talks from people in the field
  • Hands-on workshops
  • Field trips
  • Programming contests
